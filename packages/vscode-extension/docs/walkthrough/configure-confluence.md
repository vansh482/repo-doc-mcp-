## Connect to Confluence

You'll need three things:

1. **Base URL** — Your Confluence instance URL  
   Example: `https://yourcompany.atlassian.net/wiki`

2. **Space Key** — The short code for your space  
   Find it in: Space Settings → Space Details

3. **API Token** — Your personal Confluence token  
   Create one at: [id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security/api-tokens](https://id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security/api-tokens)

Pages will be created under the parent page you specify, restricted to your account only.
